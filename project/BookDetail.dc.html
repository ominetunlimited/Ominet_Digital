NOVEL

The Long Return

Omini Nfawa · 2026 · 312 pages

A family separated by three borders finds its way back to a single house, one letter at a time. Told across four decades and three countries, it is a novel about what a house remembers when the people who built it are gone.

EXCERPT

The house had three doors and only one of them still opened. Nkechi kept the other two locked not out of caution but out of a private arithmetic: some rooms should only be entered by the people who left through them.

AUTHOR'S NOTE

I wrote the first draft of this book over five years, in three cities, none of which appear in it by name. It began as a letter I never sent.
